What did we verify from the official Cities: Skylines II Paradox Wiki? Paradox Wiki Spec

All asset parameters, texture layouts, bitmasks, and shader pipelines were directly verified against the official Cities: Skylines II Paradox Wiki:

  • Complete Decal Layer Masking (_colossal_DecalLayerMask): The game engine calculates which surfaces project decals using bitwise addition:
    • 1 = Terrain (Natural ground, cliffs, soil)
    • 2 = Roads (Asphalt, highways, pavement)
    • 4 = Buildings (Zoned lots, signature buildings, walls)
    • 8 = Vehicles (Cars, trucks, trains, buses)
    • 16 = Creatures (Pedestrians, citizens, dogs, wildlife)
    • 32 = Props / Other (Curbs, jersey barriers, street furniture, rocks)
    Example: Terrain (1) + Roads (2) + Buildings (4) = 7. Adding Props (32) = 39.
  • Normal Map Opacity (_NormalOpacity): Documented under official Assets: Importing Decals. A float property (0.0 to 1.0) inside settings.json. Setting it to 0.0 removes normal bump height so underlying asphalt roughness shows through flat painted road stripes. Setting it to 1.0 uses full 3D relief (e.g. manhole iron rims).
  • Dual Pipeline Support: Our export package generates both decal.json / surface.json / netlane.json for the ExtraAssetsImporter mod, AND the official settings.json with "materialTemplate": "DefaultDecal" for native in-game Editor / PDX Mods imports.
  • Net Lane Spline Constraints: Netlanes are extruded line meshes. Wiki notes standard road marking decals should not exceed 5 meters in width without custom wide netlane handling, and texture orientation must match driving flow.
  • Surface Draw Order Warning: Surfaces must stay within -170 to 0 (Default: -50). Setting higher draw orders can cause ground polygons to bleed over elevated highway bridges and viaduct ramps!
How do Texture Rotation and Spline Flow align road arrows and lines? Rotation & Splines

In Cities: Skylines 2, road arrows, chevrons, and netlane curbs follow the spline drawing direction (from start node to end node):

  • Texture Rotation (0°, 90°, 180°, 270°): Applies a matrix transformation directly to the canvas rendering pipeline before generating Base Color, Normal, and Mask maps.
  • Left-Hand vs Right-Hand Traffic (LHT vs RHT): Use ⇄ Flip Horizontal to quickly mirror angled turn arrows or lane merger chevrons to match your city's driving side.
  • Netlane Spline Orientation: If road markings appear perpendicular to the road, rotate 90° CW or 270° CW to align the graphic along the extrusion axis.
When I export a package, does the app resize the textures? Export

No. The app does not resize or re-compress textures at the moment of export.

When you click "Export Asset Package (.zip)", the app takes the exact canvases currently rendered in your viewport (sized according to your chosen Target Texture Resolution: 512×512, 1024×1024, 2048×2048, or 4096×4096) and converts them straight into pristine PNG files.

Resizing happens upfront when you drop an image or adjust the Target Resolution dropdown in the sidebar—never in the background during packaging.
If I drop an image in at 512×512 and switch to 2048×2048, will I lose resolution? Resolution

It depends on the original resolution of your source file:

  • If your source image is 2048×2048 (or higher): You lose zero resolution. The app keeps your original image in its raw, full-resolution state in memory. Changing the dropdown to 2048×2048 redraws directly from your uncompressed original file straight onto the 2048 canvas.
  • If your source image was originally only 512×512: The browser smoothly interpolates (bicubic scale) the 512px image to fill the 2048px canvas. It won't look degraded, but upscaling cannot invent new microscopic camera detail.
Bonus for Procedural Normal & Mask Maps: Even if your color image was 512px, switching the target to 2048×2048 causes the procedural normal and mask generators to compute gradients across a true 2048×2048 pixel grid, resulting in much smoother normal vectors and cleaner lighting!
When I save a project (.cs2asset), does it save my full-resolution image? Save & Load

Yes! Project files (.cs2asset) preserve your base image at its true native dimensions.

When you click "Save Project (.cs2asset)" or save to the browser library:

  • The base texture is serialized at its original, native image dimensions (naturalWidth × naturalHeight), whether that is 512, 1024, 2048, or 4096.
  • Any custom normal maps and designer logos are also preserved at their original resolutions.
  • All sliders, physical meter dimensions, categories, and layer masks are stored.

When you reload the project in the future, you can freely switch target output resolutions without generational loss.

Why does the 'In-Game Lit Surface Simulation' tab only show a single image/tile? 3D Lit View

The In-Game Lit Surface Simulation tab is engineered as a focused, single-tile 1:1 lighting inspection tool:

  • It simulates how your asset looks projected onto wet roadway asphalt or terrain under Cities: Skylines 2 PBR lighting.
  • By moving your mouse across the surface, you dynamically adjust the sun vector angle to verify how your normal bump grooves catch shadows and how specular glossiness highlights the surface.
  • It intentionally displays a single unrepeated tile so you can inspect individual decal edges and fine reliefs without repeat distortion.
To inspect repeating tiles and seam lines across borders, switch to the dedicated Tiled Pattern tab!
How does the 'Tiled Pattern' tab slider and repeat system work? Tiling Slider

The Tiled Pattern tab is dedicated to verifying pattern repetition and seamless edge matching:

  • Repeated Tiles Slider (1× to 16×): Drag the glowing emerald slider or use the [−] and [+] buttons to adjust repetitions from 1 tile (1×1) up to 256 tiles (16×16).
  • Quick Presets: Instantly jump to 1×, 2×, 4×, 6×, 8×, 12×, or 16× repeats.
  • Seams Toggle: Displays high-visibility dashed cyan lines at each tile boundary, allowing you to instantly spot visual seams or misalignments.
  • Map Source Switcher: Inspect tiling on the Base Color, Normal Map, Mask Map, or the 3D Lit preview.
Can I insert my own custom _NormalMap.png and adjust bump height? Normal Maps

Yes! You can insert your own baked normal map or use auto-generated normals.

  • Insert Custom Normal: Click "Insert Custom _NormalMap..." under Section 2 to load any tangent-space normal map (from Blender, Substance Painter, Photoshop, etc.).
  • Relief Height / Depth Slider: When a custom normal is active, this slider acts as a vector multiplier:
    • 1.0× = 100% exact original relief of your normal map.
    • > 1.0× = Steeper, deeper grooves and more dramatic shadows.
    • < 1.0× = Softer, flatter relief.
  • Flip Green (Y) Axis: Cities: Skylines 2 uses OpenGL format (+Y up). If your normal map was baked in DirectX format (-Y down), check this box to invert the Y channel so bumps don't render inside-out in game!
How does Cities: Skylines 2 pack channels in _MaskMap.png? Mask Packing

Cities: Skylines 2 uses channel-packed textures for physical material properties:

  • Red (R) — Metallic: 0 = non-metallic (asphalt, concrete, rubber, paint), 255 = fully metallic (iron, steel, brass).
  • Green (G) — Unused: Always set to 0.
  • Blue (B) — Unused: Always set to 0.
  • Alpha (A) — Smoothness: 0 = completely rough/matte, 255 = mirror-smooth specular glossiness. This is the inverse of roughness (Smoothness = 1.0 - Roughness).
Modulate Mask with Decal Alpha: When checked, pixels that are transparent on the decal will also have 0 metallic and 0 smoothness in the mask, preventing ugly shiny halos on the road around your decal.
What is the difference between Decal, Surface, and Net Lane modes? Asset Types

Each mode targets a different asset class supported by ExtraAssetsImporter in Cities: Skylines 2:

  • Decal Mode: Projected onto terrain, roads, or building walls via an oriented 3D bounding box (Width × Height × Depth in meters). Ideal for manhole covers, arrows, road markings, stains, and graffiti. Supports transparency.
  • Surface Mode: Painted with the surface tool as infinite, tileable terrain ground covers. Requires seamless textures without transparent borders. Configured with uvScale (tiling density) and category (Pavement, Cobblestones, Asphalt, Grass, Dirt, Industrial).
  • Net Lane Mode: Extruded along curves, roads, and networks. Configured with width (stripe/curb thickness in meters) and length (texture stretch distance before repeating). Ideal for highway lane markings, double yellow lines, curbs, guardrails, and train tracks.
How does Render Draw Order Priority work? Draw Order

Draw Order controls which asset renders on top when multiple decals or surfaces overlap in the same location:

  • Decals: Range 0 to 200 (Default: 50). Higher numbers render on top of lower numbers (e.g. fresh paint over an oil stain).
  • Surfaces: Range -170 to 0 (Default: -50). Surfaces render beneath decals but above base terrain layers.
  • Net Lanes: Range 0 to 200 (Default: 60). Higher numbers render over base road pavement and lower markings.
Where do I extract my exported package files for Cities: Skylines 2? Installation

Extract your exported .zip package directly into the corresponding ExtraAssetsImporter mod directory:

Custom Decals:

%AppData%\..\LocalLow\Colossal Order\Cities Skylines II\Mods\ExtraAssetsImporter\CustomDecals\<Asset_Name>\

Custom Surfaces:

%AppData%\..\LocalLow\Colossal Order\Cities Skylines II\Mods\ExtraAssetsImporter\CustomSurfaces\<Category>\<Asset_Name>\

Custom Net Lanes:

%AppData%\..\LocalLow\Colossal Order\Cities Skylines II\Mods\ExtraAssetsImporter\CustomNetlanes\<Category>\<Asset_Name>\

In-Game Access:

  1. Make sure the ExtraAssetsImporter mod is installed via Paradox Mods.
  2. Launch Cities: Skylines 2.
  3. Open the ExtraAssetsImporter tool menu in-game to select and place your new custom decal, surface, or net lane!
How do Dual Badges (Creator + Series) and Default Watermarks work? Dual Badges

Cities: Skylines 2 UI thumbnails (_Icon.png) can now feature TWO separate overlays simultaneously:

  • 1. Creator Logo / Author Watermark: Displays your brand, studio crest, or verified author mark (⭐ Star, ✓ Verified, ⚡ CS2 Mod, 👑 Studio, 🛡️ Crest, or your custom PNG logo). Typically placed in the bottom-right corner.
  • 2. Series / Collection Badge: Identifies your pack or release category (🎨 Graffiti, 🖼️ Posters, 🛣️ Transit, 🏙️ Props, ⚠️ Hazard, 🌿 Nature, or Custom Text with 8 color themes, Google Fonts, point sizing, and letter spacing like "UK ROADS", "SPEED BUMPS", "METRO", or "PACK 01"). Typically placed in top-center or top-left.
  • Smart Collision Prevention: Even if both badges are assigned to the same corner, the generator automatically calculates an offset so neither badge covers the other.
  • Save as Default (No Reloading Needed!): In the Defaults tab, click "Save Current Badges as Default". Both logos, custom text, presets, color themes, scales, opacities, and positions are stored in browser memory and automatically apply to every new decal or session!
